    For first-time bookers

    Quick answers

    Should I ask the Deaf person what support they prefer?

    Yes. Communication needs vary. Ask the person what communication support works best for them rather than assuming.

    Do I always need two interpreters?

    No fixed answer suits every booking. Duration, complexity, breaks, setting and performance demands can all matter. Give the provider full details so they can advise appropriately.

    Why send slides or an agenda beforehand?

    Preparation material can help an interpreter understand names, terminology, context and the structure of the session.

    What if the event is being livestreamed?

    Tell the interpreting provider before confirming the booking. Filming, broadcast position, preparation and usage of the interpreter’s image may need to be agreed.
